DOSA

The traditional south indian dish dosa is made from a fermented batter of rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds.

Ingredients

  • Rice-2cups
  • Urad dal-1/2 cup
  • Fenugreek seeds-1teaspoon
  • Salt-1 to 1.5 teaspoons
  • Poha-2to3 tablespoons
  • Cooked rice-1-2 tablespoons
  • Oil or Ghee
  1. Soaking and grinding: Soak rice and urad dal separately for 4–6 hours. Grind to a smooth paste and mix to form a uniform batter.
  2. Fermentation: Allow the batter to ferment overnight in a warm place. Fermentation makes the dosa airy, improves taste, and enhances digestibility.
  3. Consistency: After fermentation, adjust the batter with water to a pourable consistency similar to pancake batter.
  4. Cooking: Heat a non-stick tawa or cast-iron skillet, pour batter, spread evenly, and cook with minimal oil until golden and crisp.
